Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

AIChatbot ("we", "our", "the service") provides an automated messaging assistant that helps businesses reply to their customers on Instagram Direct Messages. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Instagram Business Account ID and username of connected accounts
  • Access tokens needed to send and receive messages on behalf of the connected account
  • Text content of incoming and outgoing Direct Messages, used to generate replies

How We Use Data

  • To generate and send automated replies to customer messages
  • To operate and improve the quality of the messaging service

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data may be processed by service providers we use to run the service (such as hosting and AI processing providers), only as needed to provide the service.

Data Retention

Message data is stored only as long as needed to provide the service, or as required by law.

Data Deletion

Security
